Three outcomes, never blurred together

A breach lookup can end in three different ways. This site keeps them visibly separate, with plain labels instead of colour alone.

  • Listed in a documented breach

    A named source returned a record for this address. You see which breach, when, what kind of data was exposed, and a link to the source where one exists.

  • 0 known breaches found

    The source explicitly reported no record. This is not a promise of safety: breaches can be unreported, unindexed or outside the source's coverage.

  • Unavailable or inconclusive

    The lookup could not give an answer: no provider configured, a network error, a rate limit or a response we could not verify. It says nothing either way.

How this site treats your data

  • No accounts, no historySearches are not saved and there is nowhere to log in.
  • No analytics or cookiesThis site loads no tracking scripts, fonts or images from other sites.
  • No invented resultsResults come only from a configured provider and are checked before display.
  • No secrets in the pageThe free lookup needs no key. Any optional paid key lives only on the server.
