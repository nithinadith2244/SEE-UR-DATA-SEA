Limitations

Breach checking is useful, and it is also incomplete by nature. Please read these limits before relying on a result.

  • No match is not safe. Many breaches are never made public, and public ones may not be in the source's records yet.
  • Coverage is only what the source covers. This site makes no claim of worldwide or complete coverage.
  • Records are reported, not independently verified. Dates, counts and descriptions come from the provider and may be incomplete or out of date.
  • Email only. This site does not check phone numbers, passwords, usernames or other identifiers.
  • Passwords are never requested. Never type a password or code into this site.
  • One address per check, with request limits that protect the provider and your own allowance.
  • The site cannot remove data from third-party breach collections or from the internet.
  • Availability depends on a third party and on server setup. If the provider is down, rate-limited or the server has no key, you get "unavailable" or "not configured", never a guess.
  • Not professional advice. A result is information only.
